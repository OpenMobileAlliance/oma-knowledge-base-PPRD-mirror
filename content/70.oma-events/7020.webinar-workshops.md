# OMA WORKSHOPS

The **Open Mobile Alliance (OMA)** hosts a series of workshops to provide **in-depth insights and practical guidance** on implementing **LwM2M standards** in various sectors, including utilities and smart cities. These workshops offer an opportunity to learn from **industry experts**, explore real-world use cases, and get updates on the latest developments in **IoT standardization**.

**Help shape how the water industry tests for interoperability.**

Water utilities are deploying metering, network monitoring and leakage detection at scale, yet devices from different vendors still do not speak the same language. Join the **OMA Utility Working Group** online to review the **LwM2M Enabler Test Specification** for water and agree what the group will validate in the events that follow.

**In this session you will discover:**

- Why LwM2M is becoming the interoperability layer for water utility deployments
- How **Water Utilities**, such as [SUEZ](https://www.suez.com/){:target="_blank"} ..., are approaching interoperability in practice
- What the Utility Working Group will validate against the Enabler Test Specification, and on what timeline
- How your organization can join the work and influence what gets tested

The session from 10:00 CEST is a **closed session for OMA members only**.
