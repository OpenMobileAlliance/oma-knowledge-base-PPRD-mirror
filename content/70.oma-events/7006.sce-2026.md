  The Open Mobile Alliance returns to **Smart City Expo World Congress** in Barcelona with its members to show how **LwM2M** turns city and utility devices into interoperable, manageable, long-life assets. From street lighting to smart water meters, OMA standards help cities and utilities avoid vendor lock-in and deploy with confidence.

  This year we bring three things to Barcelona: **live multi-vendor demos** at the OMA stand, a dedicated **Water 2.0 panel** on the latest work of the OMA Utility Working Group, and **Smart City SIG sessions** at the Madrid Pavilion, presenting real municipal use cases developed with Madrid Digital. Come and meet us.

**Hall 3, Level 0, Street F, Stand 90** — in the same hall as the Madrid Pavilion (Street D, Stand 90).

- **Live member demos.** See LwM2M in action with OMA members [CONFIRM final list: AVSystem, Paradox Engineering, Itron]. Demo slots run throughout all three days.
- **LwM2M Client Conformance Tool.** Find out how to test your LwM2M client against the OMA specifications and link your results to your product listing.
- **Water 2.0.** Get an early look at the vendor-neutral conformance profile for smart water metering, with its first release planned for March 2027.
- **Talk to the team.** Pick up our LwM2M, Smart City and Water 2.0 flyers and meet OMA staff and member experts.

## Water 2.0 panel

### From Pilots to City-Scale: Standards-Based Connectivity for Water Management

**3 November 2026, 13:05–13:50 · Enabling Technologies session · AI X Cities stage**

Following the October 2026 LwM2M Water Interoperability Readiness Workshop, the OMA Utility Working Group brings the smart water metering conversation to Barcelona. The panel covers why utilities need multi-vendor interoperability, how to manage meters over a 15–20 year lifecycle, and where **Water 2.0** stands on its path to a first release in **March 2027**.
