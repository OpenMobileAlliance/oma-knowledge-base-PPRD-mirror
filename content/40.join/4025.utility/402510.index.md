# UTILITY MEMBERSHIP

## Join OMA and Help Shape Water 2.0

A direct path for regulated water utilities to take part in the requirements that member companies are already implementing and deploying against.

Water utilities are adding smart meters, leak sensors, water-quality monitors, and digital twins from a growing number of vendors. Each new vendor typically means new integration work, and utility teams end up repeating that work every time they add another device.

Water 2.0 is a vendor-neutral interoperability profile for smart water metering, built on the LwM2M protocol already used across millions of IoT devices worldwide. It sets out which data objects and characteristics an interoperable water meter deployment should support. It does not define or modify LwM2M itself.

This membership pathway lets investor-owned and other regulated water utilities join OMA directly, at no cost in year one, and take part in the working group writing these requirements right now, rather than adopting a standard someone else already finished.

## Who Qualifies

Investor-owned and other regulated water utilities, any size, in any country. Eligibility is verified once at signup, with no annual re-verification.

## What Members Get

- Associate-level rights to propose and vote on Water 2.0 work items
- Full participation rights across every other OMA working group
- Eligible to run for Water 2.0 Vice Chair at any time
- Eligible to run for Water 2.0 Chair during the first 12 months of membership. After that, Chair eligibility requires upgrading to Strategic or Essential Membership

## What's Expected

- A named delegate who actively contributes operational requirements
- Live working-group call attendance, preferred but not required
- Regular contributions or pull requests to the working group's GitHub repositories, a solid alternative for delegates whose time zone makes live calls difficult
- A signed membership application, not a compliance mandate

## Fees and Timeline

Year 1: Fully Waived.

Year 2 (Prorated): On your 12-month anniversary, OMA will issue a prorated invoice for the remainder of the calendar year at the current Associate Membership rate ($4,500/year). This aligns your account with OMA’s standard annual billing cycle.

Year 3 onwards: Membership renews annually on January 1 at the standard Associate rate.

Note: Because your initial fee is waived for a full 12 months, joining at any point in the calendar year guarantees you receive the complete year-one benefit.

## How to Join

Confirm eligibility first: investor-owned or other regulated water utility, any size, any country. Then choose either path below.

### Digital Submission

- Sign the OMA Water Utility Member Application Form and Agreement electronically through <a href="https://na4.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=88221b26-5af2-4b82-ac71-2a187465827f&env=na4&acct=5cb7d7a3-7be2-4e98-ae3c-bb2cc681bfd4&v=2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DocuSign</a>.
- Pay your membership dues. Year one is fully waived under the Water Utility Program. After that, dues follow OMA's current standard Associate Membership rate of $4,500 a year, billed at the 12-month anniversary of joining to align with OMA's calendar-year cycle.

### Manual Submission

- Complete the OMA Water <a href="/documentation/Membership/OMA-Reference-2026-0001-Utility.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Utility Member Application Form and Agreement</a> by hand, including your primary contact, delegate name and email, and delegate GitHub handle for working-group repository access.
- Agree to OMA's <a href="https://www.openmobilealliance.org/documents/Website/Membership/OMA-Reference-2024-0002-Bylaws.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bylaws</a>.
- Send your completed application to Open Mobile Alliance, 2907 Shelter Island Drive, Suite 105-273, San Diego, California 92106, USA.
- Email a copy to [snewberry@omaorg.org](mailto:snewberry@omaorg.org).

Once processed, your delegate gets working-group access and can start contributing to Water 2.0 requirements right away.
