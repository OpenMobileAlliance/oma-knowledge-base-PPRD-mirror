# UTILITY MEMBERSHIP

OMA offers a **dedicated membership pathway for water utilities** that want to help shape the **Water 2.0 interoperability profile**. This pathway is designed for utilities that **operate or regulate water metering systems**.

Water 2.0 defines a **vendor-neutral interoperability profile** for smart water metering, built on the **LwM2M protocol**. It identifies which **data objects and characteristics** an interoperable water meter deployment should support — it does **not** define or modify the underlying LwM2M protocol itself.

## WHO QUALIFIES

- **Water utilities that operate or regulate water metering systems**, including investor-owned, other regulated, municipal, and other public-sector utilities.
- **Utilities of any size, in any country.**

OMA verifies eligibility as part of the application. There is **no annual re-verification requirement** once a utility qualifies.

## WORKING GROUP CHAIR ELIGIBILITY — PROGRAM YEAR EXCEPTION

::ShAlert
---
typeAlert: warning
---
During the **Program Year only**, a member participating in the Utility Program is eligible for election as **Chair** of the **Water 2.0 Working Group** — an exception to OMA's standard rule that Associate Members are eligible only for **Vice-Chair**, not Chair.
<br><br>
<i>This exception ends automatically at the earlier of: the **Program Anniversary** (the 12-month anniversary of the member's acceptance), or the utility's **transition to standard Associate billing**. After that point, a member participating in the Water Utility Program is eligible for election as **Water 2.0 Vice-Chair only**, on the same terms as any other Associate Member. A utility that wishes to retain or regain Chair eligibility after the Program Year must **upgrade to Strategic or Essential Membership**.</i>
::
